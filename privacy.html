Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Demo policy text

This is placeholder policy text for the demo site. Replace it with legally reviewed content before going live.

1. Information we collect

On this demo website, details you enter in forms (contact, newsletter, consultation and checkout) and items you add to your cart are stored only in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to a server.

2. How we use information

Collected demo data is used only to display confirmations and to let you test the storefront and admin panel. A production site would use this information to process orders, respond to enquiries and improve service.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage for demo functionality: cart contents, saved orders, form submissions and admin product edits. You can clear these at any time from your browser settings.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell or share personal information. In a live deployment, payment and delivery partners may receive the minimum data required to fulfil an order.

5. Your choices

You may clear browser storage, unsubscribe from newsletters (in a live system) or contact us with privacy requests.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@homeokit.example.
